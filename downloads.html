iPhone installation guidance

  1. Only download an IPA from a release link verified by the port creator.
  2. Use a trusted signing/install method compatible with your Apple account and iOS version; an unsigned IPA cannot be installed directly.
  3. Follow the installation tool's instructions to sign the app and trust its developer profile when required.
  4. Launch the app, test menus, songs and touch controls, and report issues.

Availability, signing requirements and installation options vary. No unofficial signing service is endorsed here.
