The project

THE ORIGINAL ATMOSPHERE. ON iOS.

Built around the original mod's artwork, charts, audio, cinematic moments and custom effects. This port adapts the experience for touchscreen play while working toward faithful PC-style presentation.

Original content

Mod assets, charts, music and visual identity integrated into the mobile project.

Touch-first gameplay

THE TAPE IS STILL PLAYING.

Some memories refuse to fade. Beneath the grain of an old recording, a familiar world returns — its music distorted, its streets deserted, and its past waiting to be remembered.

Follow the three chapters of TAPE 1 as the atmosphere grows darker. Each song takes the story further into the unsettling world of Sunday Night Suicide: Rookies Edition V1.
